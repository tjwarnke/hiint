Notes on the current state of the game:
The game has a freezing issue as it continues to run, so the best way to enjoy the game is to test all the puzzling features and dialogue seperately. first, go to the chair in the dining room for the character 4
introductions then retrace your steps to the first chair to guess the killer.
Then, restart your game and go as fast as you can through the everything, skipping all the dialogue, Once you get the 3 books in the library, consider that puzzle finished and hit the lever on the far right
Finish the billiards room puzzle (press e on the billiards table with the 8-ball). Note be on the lookout for small levers in the billiards room. And head to the dungeon. The 3 lever puzzle at the top is not animated so it is hard to tell what to do, so just press E on the second
and third lever (left is 1st, middle is 2nd, right is 3rd). If the game is freezing too bad it may need to be restarted and played as fast as possible until you get back to where you are.











--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------Puzzle Solutions --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
library:
  jump to the first bookshelf select option 2
  jump from secret platform to 2nd bookshelf, select option 1
  (not working) put the books in the order of the birth of the kusnetzov siblings, hinted by the bookshelves (nana, vlas, anna)
  (working) just flip the lever on the far right side of the room
Billiards:
  Jump on chandelier to the window pane, flip the lever to reveal the safe behind the painting
  Jump to the other side and flip the lever on the window pane, turning on the light and revealing the code
  Open the safe with the code (option 2) and take the 8 ball
  press e on the billiards table while holding the 8 ball
Dungeon:
  Use the hammer to break the glass at the top of the room
  The armor is holding the sword in the direction the levers should flip (2nd and 3rd levers need to flip)
  Backtrack to the first room, a hole has appeared in the floor. 
  Get the key to open the last door and head to the ladder to escape the dungeon
